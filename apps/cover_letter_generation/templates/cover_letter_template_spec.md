# Cover letter template (ganesha-cover-v1)

Ganesha renders the letter with python-docx and converts it through the existing Word, then LibreOffice, path. The checked-in layout is the renderer in `cover_letter_docx_render_service.py`, not a LaTeX file.

## Page

- Size: U.S. Letter (8.5 by 11 inches)
- Margins: 0.85 in top, 0.8 in bottom, 0.9 in left and right
- One column
- No tables, text boxes, images, icons, or page numbers
- No footer and no decorative heading such as COVER LETTER

## Type

- Family: Calibri
- Name: 16 pt, bold, dark blue `#1F4E79`, centered
- Contact: 10 pt, centered, verified contact line only
- Body, date, salutation, and signature: 11 pt
- Line spacing: 1.08
- Space after body paragraphs: 8 pt

## Structure

1. Candidate name
2. Contact line, when a verified contact fact exists
3. Date (`Month D, YYYY`)
4. `Dear [Company Name] Hiring Team,`
5. Three paragraphs
6. `Sincerely,`
7. `Alex Michael Manjakunnel`

The header does not include a target role or company. The company appears in the salutation and the body.

## Length

- Preferred body length: 250–325 words
- Accepted body length: 140–350 words (prefer ~250–325 when evidence supports it; do not pad)
- Exactly one page after PDF conversion
